🔬 Methodology

Energy Estimation: BMR calculated using Mifflin-St Jeor (1990), multiplied by activity factor. Goal adjustment (±300–500 kcal) applied on top.

Macronutrients: Protein: 0.8–1.2g/kg body weight. Carbs: 50–60% total energy. Fat: 25–35% total energy. Adjusted for activity level.

Micronutrients: RDA values from ICMR-NIN 2020 for Indian adults. Where Indian values unavailable, WHO/FAO DRI used.

Food Data: Per-100g nutritional values sourced from IFCT 2017 and USDA FoodData Central, averaged where multiple entries exist.
